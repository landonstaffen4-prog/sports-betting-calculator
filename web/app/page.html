What the application will not infer

  • A market move proves informed money.
  • Three brands mean three independent opinions.
  • A high hit rate means positive expected value.
  • A headline injury has a known numerical impact.
  • Calibration, a winning streak or positive closing value guarantees profit.

Game conditions can invalidate old prices. They are not added twice to a market estimate or translated into invented player projections.
